No skip/xfail, no tautological expectations, no historical RED claim from regenerated fixtures. Native collection must equal the union of three unique whole-module shards; source/schedule tampering and missing phase evidence must fail. Local draft evidence is not hosted regression certification.

Full App regression, remote CI, publication and branch/ruleset changes need independent authority. Stop for real engineering uncertainty, unsupported migration, unsafe content/isolation, unresolved meaningful failure, expired cost authority or any unauthorized mutation. Keep all originals/recovery; never destructively restart. After local review, stop before publication.
